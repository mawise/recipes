# Lentil Sloppy Joes

## Ingredients

The Base: 1 cup dry brown or green lentils (or 2 cans cooked lentils, rinsed)  
Veggies: 1 yellow onion, 1 green bell pepper (or celery), 3 cloves garlic (all minced)  
The Sauce: 
 - 1 cup ketchup (or 1 15-oz can crushed tomatoes + ¼ cup ketchup)  
 - 1 Tbsp brown sugar or maple syrup  
 - 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar  
 - 1 Tbsp vegetarian Worcestershire sauce (or soy sauce)  
 - 1 Tbsp Dijon or yellow mustard  

Spices: 1 Tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt and pepper to taste  

For Serving: Hamburger buns and dill pickles  

## Instructions

1. Cook the Lentils: Skip if using canned lentils. Bring 3 cups of water to a boil in a medium pot. Add the dry lentils, reduce heat to a simmer, and cook uncovered for 15-20 minutes until tender but not mushy. Drain and set aside.
2. Sauté the Aromatics: Heat 1-2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the minced onion and bell pepper. Cook for 5-7 minutes until soft, then stir in the garlic, chili powder, and smoked paprika for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Build the Sauce: Pour in the ketchup, brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, and mustard. Stir well to combine the sauce with the vegetable base.
4. Simmer and Thicken: Fold the cooked lentils into the sauce. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer and let it cook for 10-15 minutes. This allows the lentils to absorb the flavors and the sauce to thicken into that classic Sloppy Joe consistency.
5. Serve: Toast your hamburger buns. Scoop a generous amount of the lentil mixture onto the bottom bun, top with dill pickle slices, and crown with the top bun.
